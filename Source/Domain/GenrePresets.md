# Organic House Preset Design

Organic House is consistently described as a warmer, slower and more
atmospheric house style with a steady four-on-the-floor base, hand percussion,
subtle swing, acoustic/natural textures and repetitive but evolving melodic
material. Typical references cluster around approximately 110–125 BPM.
